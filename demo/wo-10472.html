Long Description

Condition alert raised by the building digital twin. Supply airflow on PIU_01_11 is trending below setpoint: delivered air measured ~552 CFM against a desired ~624 CFM, holding below the 560 CFM lower warn limit for a sustained interval. Fan commanded on; damper driven to ~86% open; the airflow deficit persists while space temperature remains within the comfort band. Proactive service requested before occupant impact.
  1. Inspect supply fan & ECM motor; verify commanded vs. actual speed.
  2. Check filter and coil for obstruction / fouling; clear as needed.
  3. Verify airflow sensor calibration against a reference reading.
  4. Inspect damper actuator & linkage; confirm full stroke.
